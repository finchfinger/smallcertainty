## BATH

### Best Bath Towel

**Abyss Super Pile**

Abyss Super Pile makes abundance useful. It is thick and unusually soft, but the pleasure does not come at the expense of absorption or durability. Many luxury towels feel impressive on a shelf and cumbersome when wet. Super Pile has the density expected of a serious hotel towel, yet its long cotton loops dry the body efficiently and recover their loft through repeated washing.

The towel is made in Portugal from 100 percent Egyptian extra-long-staple cotton. Abyss uses considerably more cotton than an ordinary household towel, creating a deep pile with a smooth hand and strong absorbency. Rounded corners and hand-finished piping keep the edges from becoming stiff, while the bath size measures approximately 70 by 140 centimeters. A palette of roughly 40 colors makes white only the beginning rather than the default answer.

That weight has consequences. Super Pile takes longer to dry than gauze, occupies more room in the machine, and performs best without fabric softener, which can reduce absorbency. It also rewards a ventilated bathroom. In exchange, the towel feels substantial after every bath, tolerates frequent laundering, and does not lose its authority after a few months of use.

The best towel should not need decorative borders or an inflated scale to suggest quality. Super Pile earns its place through cotton, construction, and the reassuring way it works each day. Choose a color with conviction, buy enough to maintain a sensible washing rotation, and let the material age. It is a simple household textile made with uncommon seriousness, and there is little reason to continue comparing.

### Best Shower Curtain

**Quiet Town Sun Shower**

Quiet Town's Sun Shower treats a necessary waterproof sheet as part of the room. Its translucent color alters daylight, gives privacy without creating a wall, and brings more life to a bathroom than another white textile liner. The effect is immediate but not frivolous. This is color doing architectural work in a place usually surrendered to plumbing and pale tile.

The curtain is made from heavy 12-gauge EVA, now incorporating 30 percent recycled material. EVA contains no chlorine, PVC, or BPA, and Quiet Town's substantial sheet avoids the clammy tendency of very thin liners. Rounded lower corners soften the outline, while solid brass grommets, finished in nickel or brass, provide durable hanging points. At 72 inches square, it fits a conventional tub and can be used alone.

Transparency changes with the chosen color, folds, and available light, so samples on a bright screen are only a guide. Soap residue remains visible and should be wiped away with a gentle cleaner before it builds up. Heat above 120 degrees Fahrenheit can damage the material. Those constraints are modest, and the curtain avoids laundering, dries quickly, and stays in place more convincingly than a disposable vinyl liner.

A shower curtain is a large uninterrupted surface, whether the room acknowledges it or not. Sun Shower uses that fact with uncommon economy. One material provides water protection, privacy, color, and a controlled glow, while the hardware is good enough to keep. Select the shade in relation to daylight rather than towels, and the bathroom gains atmosphere without acquiring another decorative theme. That is a small, decisive improvement.

### Best Showerhead

**Speakman Signature Icon Anystream**

The Speakman Signature Icon Anystream concentrates on pressure, coverage, and adjustment instead of becoming an overhead lighting fixture. Its compact form belongs to the long American tradition of durable commercial bathroom hardware. The Icon looks almost severe beside fashionable rain heads, but the first shower explains the judgment: water arrives with purpose, and the pattern can be changed without deciphering controls.

The solid-brass body contains eight precision jets that divide the flow into 64 spray channels. Rotating the side lever moves continuously between a concentrated needling spray, a broader rain pattern, and a fuller flood setting. Speakman's Anystream mechanism turns through 360 degrees and helps clear mineral buildup as it moves. Versions are offered at several flow rates and in finishes ranging from polished chrome to aged brass.

The Icon cannot imitate the enveloping scale of a ceiling-mounted rain shower, and its force may be excessive for anyone who prefers a very soft spray. Finish and local water hardness will determine how often the face needs attention. Installation on a standard half-inch connection is straightforward, the adjustment can be made with wet hands, and the brass construction avoids the cracked plastic joints found in lighter fixtures.

A showerhead is successful when inconsistent household water begins to feel deliberate. Speakman's answer is mechanical, compact, and repairable enough to have credibility beyond a renovation photograph. The lever supplies meaningful variation, the jets preserve pressure, and the object occupies no more space than its task requires. It is not the newest idea in the bathroom. It is the mature version of a good one.

### Best Bathroom Faucet

**Vola HV1**

The Vola HV1’s simplicity survives contact with actual use. Arne Jacobsen designed it in 1968 from cylinders, circles, and one slim lever, establishing a form that has since become almost generic through imitation. The original remains convincing because the proportions are exact, the operation is immediately clear, and the apparent reduction conceals manufacturing of unusual precision.

A ceramic-disc cartridge controls the single lever, while a fixed spout ends in a water-saving aerator. The standard body stands 120 millimeters high, with alternate spout, lever, and body lengths available through Vola's modular system. Components are hand-soldered so that no joint interrupts the surface. Brass or stainless-steel construction supports a wide range of hand-applied colors and metallic finishes, many of which can be serviced rather than discarded.

The short standard form is best matched to a modest basin with correctly planned reach. Splashing is usually a problem of specification, not the faucet itself, so basin depth, hole position, and flow rate deserve attention before ordering. Painted finishes will show careless cleaning, while natural brass is intended to patinate. In daily use, the lever is precise, the aerated stream is controlled, and the uncluttered body is easy to wipe.

Few bathroom fittings need to announce themselves, but anonymity is not the same as restraint. HV1 has enough character to organize a basin and enough discipline to remain there for decades. Its geometry accepts color without becoming novelty, and its replaceable internal parts turn longevity into a practical claim. Specify it correctly, choose the finish for the room rather than the moment, and stop searching.

### Best Bathtub

**Kaldewei Meisterstück Centro Duo Oval**

The Kaldewei Meisterstück Centro Duo Oval combines the presence of a freestanding bath with the discipline of an engineered fixture. Its exterior is a calm, continuous oval rather than an ornamental shell, and its interior gives equal consideration to either end. It can anchor a large bathroom, but the design remains about bathing rather than producing a sculptural object that happens to hold water.

Designed with Sottsass Associati, the tub is formed from Kaldewei steel enamel, joining a rigid steel body to a hard, glasslike surface. The molded exterior panel is seamless, and the waste cover and overflow control are enamelled to sit quietly within the composition. Two identical sloping backrests flank a centered waste. Sizes include 170 by 75 and 180 by 80 centimeters, both with a generous interior depth.

Steel enamel feels cooler on first contact than acrylic and demands care with dropped metal objects, although its surface resists ordinary scratching, staining, and discoloration exceptionally well. The tub's weight, floor connections, and freestanding position require planning before finishes are complete. Once installed, the centered outlet makes solo or shared bathing comfortable, and the nonporous interior is easier to clean than textured composite materials.

A bathtub occupies too much money, space, and construction effort to rely on a passing silhouette. Centro Duo Oval earns permanence through material clarity and balanced geometry. It is large without being swollen, luxurious without decorative signals, and suited to two people without compromising one. If the room and structure can accommodate a freestanding bath, this is the rare model whose physical and visual life justify the decision.

### Best Bathroom Sink

**Duravit Starck 1 Washbasin**

The Duravit Starck 1 Washbasin recovers the elemental authority of the basin. Philippe Starck's 1994 collection looked to buckets, bowls, and old washstands rather than the aerodynamic forms common to sanitary ware. The round ceramic vessel is immediate and familiar, but its disciplined rim and generous depth make the archetype useful in a contemporary bathroom rather than merely picturesque.

Starck 1 was conceived as a complete family of bathroom objects, and the washbasin is strongest when allowed to retain its clear circular outline. Vitreous ceramic provides a hard, nonporous surface, while versions accommodate different installations and faucet arrangements. The best-known configuration sits above the collection's conical barrel vanity, a carefully formed cabinet that turns the old washstand into a compact piece of modern furniture.

The round bowl offers less flat deck space than a conventional rectangular basin, and the faucet must be positioned carefully to prevent splash. Its curved interior, however, has no fussy corners to collect residue, and the glazed surface tolerates routine bathroom cleaning. The barrel cabinet stores necessities close at hand while preserving a smaller visual footprint than a wide vanity. This is especially persuasive in a well-planned powder room.

Bathroom design often confuses novelty with improvement. Starck 1 endures because it begins with a form people already understand, then adjusts proportion, construction, and storage with considerable care. The basin does not vanish, but neither does it demand a themed room around it. It makes washing feel like the central act rather than an excuse for cabinetry, and that directness remains difficult to better.

### Best Mirror

**GUBI F.A. 33 Mirror**

The GUBI F.A. 33’s frame contributes character without reducing the usefulness of the reflection. Gio Ponti designed it in 1933 for FontanaArte, the glass and lighting company he founded with Pietro Chiesa. The gently tapered silhouette and clipped upper corners make a rectangular mirror feel composed, but the gesture remains quiet enough for a bathroom, hall, or bedroom.

The mirror is bordered by a narrow frame in polished brass or blackened metal, with a lower edge that curves subtly before rising into the straight sides. GUBI produces it in more than one size, allowing the proportions to work above a basin or as a taller wall mirror. The construction is thin and visually light, relying on the precision of the outline rather than a deep frame or decorative molding.

Polished brass will register fingerprints and gradually change unless maintained, while blackened metal asks less attention. The shaped top requires accurate leveling because a slight error is easier to see than on a plain rectangle. Those are small obligations. The generous uninterrupted glass remains practical for daily grooming, and the narrow frame gives the wall a finished edge without stealing useful reflective area or becoming difficult to clean.

Mirrors are often selected last, as though neutrality guaranteed longevity. F.A. 33 offers a better kind of permanence. Its profile is recognizable but not mannered, its material ages intelligibly, and its usefulness is not compromised for effect. Ponti gave a necessary plane just enough architecture to hold its place in a room. Nearly a century later, that remains the right amount of design.
