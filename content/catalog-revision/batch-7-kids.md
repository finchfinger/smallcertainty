### Best Stroller

The back rail curves gently around the body and provides a grip when the chair is moved. N65 is compact and stable, but it is not a climbing frame and should be checked as bolts settle under enthusiastic use. Wipe spills promptly, tighten fixings when needed, and accept pencil marks before resorting to abrasive refinishing. The low back supports sitting without confining movement. Once outgrown at the table, the chair remains useful beside a bed, beneath a plant, or as a small stool.

Most children’s chairs are temporary because their imagery expires before their dimensions do. N65 avoids that waste through honest scale, durable birch, and a form connected to the rest of the home. It costs more than molded plastic, but repair, resale, and later uses extend the judgment. Give a child a chair that works properly, and leave imagination to what happens while sitting in it.

### Best Children’s Book

**Where the Wild Things Are**

Maurice Sendak gives anger, imagination, fear, and return their proper scale without explaining them away. Max misbehaves, sails through night and day, rules a wild rumpus, and chooses to go home. The story is brief enough to memorize, but its emotional movement remains large enough to meet a child differently across many readings.

Sendak controls the book through the changing relationship between image and page. Early illustrations sit inside generous white margins, then expand as Max’s room becomes forest, until the wild rumpus fills the spreads and words disappear. The monsters combine claws, horns, fur, and oddly human feet, making them frightening and ridiculous at once. Typography stays quiet while pacing is carried by page turns.

The language rewards reading aloud, especially its repeated sounds and disciplined sentences. Children may linger over the wordless spreads or return immediately to the beginning, and adults should allow both. The standard hardcover survives better than a delicate special edition, though any copy will show worn corners. The story does not need explanatory questions after every page; its images and final hot supper have already completed the work.

Many children’s books mistake instruction for meaning or noise for delight. Sendak trusts the reader with fury, freedom, and the comfort of being loved after both. The book is beautifully made, psychologically exact, and never longer than necessary. Buy a copy meant to be handled, read it without performing every monster excessively, and let repetition reveal why a small story can contain an entire voyage.

### Best Children’s Film

**My Neighbor Totoro**

Hayao Miyazaki understands that wonder needs ordinary life around it. Two sisters move to an old house, explore fields and woods, worry about their hospitalized mother, and encounter creatures whose existence is never overexplained. The film offers adventure without a villain and emotion without manipulation, trusting weather, waiting, and a child’s attention to carry the story.

Studio Ghibli’s hand-drawn animation gives equal care to soot sprites, wind in grass, a kitchen pump, and Totoro’s immense breathing body. Backgrounds establish a rural Japan specific enough to feel inhabited, while Joe Hisaishi’s score lifts moments without smothering them. The Catbus is exhilarating because the film has first taught us the pace of walking, bicycles, and a long wait beside a road in rain.

Very young viewers may find the mother’s illness and Mei’s disappearance upsetting, though the film handles both with honesty and reassurance. The dubbed versions are accessible, while subtitles preserve the original performances for older children. Watch it on the largest calm screen available, leave phones elsewhere, and resist explaining whether Totoro is real. Ambiguity is not a puzzle the film needs an adult to solve.

Children’s cinema often mistakes speed for attention and jokes for warmth. Totoro creates a richer form of engagement through observation, suspense, and delight in a world that remains partly unknowable. Adults find more in it without taking it away from children, which is the mark of enduring family art. Buy or stream a legitimate edition, watch together, and allow silence after the final image.
